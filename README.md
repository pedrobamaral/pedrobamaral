## Experience

### NeoLabsAI · AI Engineer · *May 2026 – present*

AI platform for risk analysis of individuals and companies, orchestrating LLM agents and human review.

- **Risk engine:** implemented 70+ risk-detection rules (judicial, corporate-structure and sanctions factors), including graph analysis of ownership networks, each with typed evidence and its regulatory basis.
- **Workflows:** built the versioned workflow engine, a graph DSL compiled to Conductor OSS that combines rules, LLM agents and human review queues with SLAs, escalation and role-based access control.
- **Agents & RAG:** developed the agent layer with Agno, with JSON Schema-validated structured output, multi-agent teams and a RAG knowledge base with a pluggable vector store (pgvector and S3 Vectors).
- **Explainability & reporting:** implemented the context and prompt that explain risk results in natural language, regulatory reports in PDF and XML, and an LLM benchmark (latency, cost and quality).
- **Platform:** implemented persistence for the serverless migration (PostgreSQL and DynamoDB with parity tests, backfill and cutover with rollback), observability with Langfuse, OpenTelemetry and Grafana, and cut backend test suite time by 26%.
